Haven Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Information We Collect

Haven Bot may collect and process information required for the bot's features, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and command-related information.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of Haven Bot, including moderation and server management features.

Data Storage

We only store information when necessary for the bot's functionality. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Deletion

Server administrators may request deletion of stored server-related data by contacting the bot owner.

Third-Party Services

Haven Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Haven Bot owner through Discord.
